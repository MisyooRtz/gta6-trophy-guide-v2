    Roadmap to the Platinum

    About 200 hours at difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx), one playthrough, no mission replay, free roam after the story. Play the five chapters, take optional missions as they appear, scan vehicles and study the world on the way — then work the 100% checklist.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy on the whole list may be missable. Everything else — story trophies included — can be earned after the credits in free roam.

    Save habit: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus.
